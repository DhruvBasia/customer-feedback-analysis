How the model reads a message

  1. 1. CleanRemove links, @mentions and punctuation; make everything lowercase.
  2. 2. Convert to numbersTF-IDF gives every word and two-word phrase a score.
  3. 3. ClassifyLogistic Regression adds up the word weights for each class.
  4. 4. ReportThe class with the highest probability is shown, with its confidence.
